How they fit together
Card to scoresheet

They were built in the order you actually use them — but each one stands on its own. Pick the one that solves your problem and ignore the rest.

01 // Sort

Rack Detector

Point it at a card full of photos and AI sorts them into per-species folders. The elk are in the elk folder before you have looked at a single frame.

02 // Cull

Rack Viewer

Rip through that folder at arrow-key speed, star the ones worth keeping, and move everything else to the trash in one action.

03 // Track

Rack Tracker

Log the keepers against the specific animal they show, and watch that bull's movement draw itself across the mountain, season after season.

04 // Score

Rack Scorer

When you finally put your hands on the rack, score it properly at the tailgate — gross and net, live, with no signal and no arithmetic.

The apps
What each one does

Rack Detector

Windows

AI that batch-sorts a season of trail-camera photos into per-species folders — surface the elk without the scrubbing.

  • Sorts thousands of images unattended into folders by species
  • Runs entirely on your own computer — no upload, no cloud
  • An NVIDIA GPU is strongly recommended. It runs CPU-only too, but much slower — use the free trial to measure it on your machine first

$100 one-time · free trial sorts up to 10,000 images

See Rack Detector → ↓ Download (Free)

Rack Viewer

Windows

Rip through a season of camera-card photos and video, keeping only the ones worth keeping.

  • Photos are decoded ahead of you, so holding the arrow key never waits
  • Press K to star a keeper; clips play with sound right in the window
  • One action moves every non-keeper to a single trash folder — moved, never deleted

Free to view, forever · $10 one-time to keep clearing out, after 10 free runs

See Rack Viewer → ↓ Download (Free)

Rack Tracker

Android

Stop guessing which bull you're looking at. Track individual animals across every season.

  • Log every trail-cam hit, glassing session and shed find against the animal it belongs to
  • Watch that rack's movement draw itself across satellite and topo maps, in order
  • Built for elk, mule deer, whitetail, bighorn and bear — and your data never leaves your phone

$10 / year · free to start, with room for a couple of racks

See Rack Tracker → Get it on Google Play ↗

Rack Scorer

Android

Score it properly at the tailgate — no folded paper sheet, no arithmetic, no signal.

  • Boone & Crockett and Pope & Young style scoring, seven species groups across thirteen categories
  • Enter measurements in inches and eighths; gross and net totals recalculate as you go
  • Save the sheet, share the summary, and check it against book minimums — fully offline

$4.99 one-time · no subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases
