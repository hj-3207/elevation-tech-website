The apps
A closer look

Rack Detector

Windows

Point at an SD card full of trail-camera photos and it filters out blanks and livestock, then sorts the photos into per-species folders. The elk are in the elk folder before you have looked at a single frame.

  • Identifies elk, mule deer, whitetail, bird, bear, human and more
  • Runs entirely on your own computer — no upload, no cloud
  • An NVIDIA GPU is strongly recommended. It runs CPU-only too, but much slower — use the free trial to measure it on your machine first

$100 one-time · includes a Rack Viewer license · free trial sorts up to 10,000 images

See Rack Detector →

Rack Viewer

Windows

Rip through a folder of photos at arrow-key speed, star the ones worth keeping, and move everything else to the trash in one action.

  • Photos are decoded ahead of you, so holding the arrow key never waits
  • Press K to star a keeper; clips play with sound right in the window
  • Non-keepers are moved, never deleted — you empty the trash folder yourself

Free to view, forever · $10 one-time to keep clearing out, after 10 free runs

See Rack Viewer →

Rack Tracker

Android

Track and map sightings throughout the year and season after season. Every trail-cam hit, find while glassing and shed scoop belongs to a named rack, so you can see a short list of your targets and observe trends and patterns.

  • Full backups, single-rack shares, and GPX import and export
  • Watch that rack's movement draw itself across satellite and topo maps, in order
  • Built for elk, mule deer, whitetail, bighorn and bear — and your data never leaves your phone

$10 / year · free to start, with room for a couple of racks

See Rack Tracker →

Rack Scorer

Android

When you put your hands on the rack, enter the measurements and have the scoresheet calculated automatically. Save and easily share with family and friends.

  • Boone & Crockett and Pope & Young style scoring, seven species groups across thirteen categories
  • Enter measurements in inches and eighths; gross and net totals recalculate as you go
  • Check it against B&C Awards, All-Time and Pope & Young minimums — fully offline

$4.99 one-time · no subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases
