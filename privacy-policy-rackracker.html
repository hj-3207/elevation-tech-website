RackTracker Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 17, 2026  •  By Elevation Technology

1. Who We Are

RackTracker is developed and operated by Elevation Technology. If you have questions about this policy, you can reach us at elevationtechnology@outlook.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum information necessary to provide the app.

Account information (collected by us):

  • Email address — required to create an account. We use this to send you a one-time sign-in link and to manage your subscription status. We do not collect a password.
  • Subscription status — we record whether your subscription is active and its expiry date so the app can verify access.

Data that stays on your device (never sent to us):

  • Animal records (Tines) — names, animal type, notes, and any attached photos you add.
  • Sighting records — date, time of day, sighting type (trail camera, in-person, shed), and notes.
  • Location records — GPS coordinates, names, and notes for your hunting spots.
  • Photos — images you attach to animals or sightings are stored locally in the app's private storage on your device.

All of this data is stored in a SQLite database on your device. It is not uploaded to our servers, backed up to the cloud, or shared with any third party unless you explicitly export it yourself.

3. Device Permissions

The app requests the following permissions. You can decline any of them; doing so will only disable the related feature.

  • Camera — to take photos directly in the app for sighting records. Only used when you tap the camera button.
  • Photo library — to attach existing photos from your device to a sighting or animal record.
  • Location (while using the app) — to auto-fill GPS coordinates when you create a new hunting location. We do not track your location in the background or log a history of where you've been.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Email address — to send you a sign-in link and to look up your subscription status when the app starts.
  • Subscription status — to determine whether to grant access to full app features or show the demo mode.

We do not use your information for advertising, behavioral profiling, or sale to third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase (privacy policy) — our backend provider. Your email address and subscription record are stored on Supabase-managed infrastructure. Supabase does not have access to your hunting data.
  • OpenStreetMap (privacy policy) — map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap servers to display the in-app map. Standard web request metadata (IP address, tile coordinates) may be logged by OpenStreetMap when map tiles are fetched.

We do not use any analytics services, advertising networks, or crash-reporting tools that collect personal data.

6. Data Export and Portability

You own your data. The app provides built-in export tools:

  • JSON export — exports all your animals, sightings, locations, and photos as a single file you can save or share.
  • GPX export — exports your hunting locations as a GPX waypoint file compatible with GPS devices and mapping apps.

Exported files go wherever you choose to save them (your Files app, AirDrop, email, etc.). We have no visibility into what you do with exported data.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • On-device data — deleted when you delete the app or clear its data through your device settings.
  • Account data (email and subscription record) — retained for as long as your account exists. To request deletion of your account and associated data, email us at elevationtechnology@outlook.com and we will remove your record within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

RackTracker is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Security

We use passwordless authentication (one-time email codes) and the PKCE security flow so that no password is ever stored or transmitted. Your account data is stored on Supabase, which employs industry-standard security practices. Your hunting data never leaves your device, so it is protected by your device's own security (screen lock, encryption, etc.).

10. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we may also notify you via the email address associated with your account.

11. Contact
