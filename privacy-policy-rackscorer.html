Rack Scorer: Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026 · Applies to Rack Scorer 2.1.2 and later

The short version

  • Rack Scorer collects no data whatsoever. Not your name, not your email, not your location, not usage statistics. Nothing.
  • Your scoresheets never leave your device unless you export or share one yourself.
  • There is no account, no sign-up, and no server behind the app.
  • The app has no networking code in it. It works with the radio off, which is the point.

Who we are

Rack Scorer is developed and operated by Elevation Technology Solutions ("we," "us"). Questions about this policy can go to elevationtechnology@outlook.com.

What we collect

Nothing. We have no analytics, no advertising software, no crash reporting, no telemetry, and no account system. We do not know who installs the app, how often it is opened, or what you score with it.

This is a property of how the app is built rather than a promise about how we behave: Rack Scorer contains no code that sends data anywhere. There is no backend service associated with it.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter is written to your phone's local app storage and stays there:

  • Scoresheets: every measurement you record, along with gross and net totals.
  • Sheet details: the hunter name, date and unit you type in, plus species and subspecies.
  • Saved score history: the full list of sheets you have kept.

None of this is transmitted, backed up to us, or accessible to us in any way.

Worth knowing: because your sheets exist only on your device, uninstalling the app or wiping the phone erases them permanently. We hold no backup and cannot restore them. Export the sheets that matter to you.

Purchases

Rack Scorer is a paid app. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play: your name, payment details and billing address are entered into Google Play, never into Rack Scorer. We receive no payment information. Google provides us only with aggregate sales reporting, which does not identify individual buyers to us.

The app itself contains no billing or subscription software, and makes no purchase-verification calls of its own.

Sharing and exporting

Rack Scorer can export a completed scoresheet as a summary image, and can share a scoresheet file to another device. Both happen only when you choose them, and the result goes wherever you send it (your gallery, a messaging app, email) using your device's own sharing features. Nothing passes through us, and we have no visibility into it.

Once a file leaves the app it is outside our control and outside this policy. A shared sheet contains whatever you typed into it, including the hunter name and unit, so treat it the way you would any personal record.

Device permissions

Rack Scorer requests no runtime permissions for its core function. Saving or sharing an exported image uses your device's standard file and share dialogs, which you control at the moment you use them. The app does not request location, camera, contacts or microphone access.

Data retention and deletion

  • Your scoresheets: held only on your device; deleted individually in the app, or all at once by uninstalling. There is nothing to request from us, because we hold no copy.
  • Purchase records: retained by Google Play under its own policy. Refunds and purchase history are managed through Google Play.

Children's privacy

Rack Scorer is not directed at children under 13. Since the app collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing for us to hold or delete, but if you have a concern, please contact us.

Security

Your scoresheets never travel over a network, which eliminates the most common way such data is exposed. On the device they are protected by the operating system's app sandboxing and by your own screen lock and device encryption.

About this website

Everything above describes the app. This website is separate, and being a website it cannot make the same absolute claim.

  • These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages (privacy statement), which logs requests to serve them, as any web host does.
  • The Rack Scorer page embeds a demo video from YouTube. We use the youtube-nocookie.com player, which avoids setting tracking cookies unless you press play; once you do, YouTube's own terms and privacy policy apply to that playback.
  • We run no analytics on this site and set no cookies of our own.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we will update the date at the top of this page. If a future version of Rack Scorer ever begins collecting anything (cloud backup of your sheets, for instance), we will say so here before that version ships.
