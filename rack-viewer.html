How it works
Open a folder. Keep the good ones. Bin the rest.

Sorting your photos by species is one job. Deciding which ones are actually worth keeping is another — and that one is still yours. Rack Viewer just makes it fast.

01 // BROWSE

Never wait on a photo

Photos are decoded ahead of where you are, in the direction you're going, so holding the arrow key feels instant even on full-size camera JPEGs.

02 // KEEP

One key per photo

Press K on anything worth keeping. A gold star marks it on the image and it drops into the keeper list, so you always know where you stand.

03 // CLEAR OUT

Move the rest in one go

One Keeper Cleanup moves everything that isn't a keeper to one trash folder, grouped by where it came from. Nothing is deleted — you empty it yourself when you're happy.

A look at it
Everything on one screen

What's in it
Built for one job, done properly

Everything below works with no internet connection and no sign-in. The one exception is activating a license, which happens once.

VIDEO

Clips play with sound

Play, pause, scrub, step a frame at a time, and adjust the volume without leaving the window. Audio and picture stay locked together.

DETAIL

Zoom in on the rack

Ctrl and the scroll wheel zooms straight in on the pointer, and you can drag to pan. Works on a paused video frame too.

FULL SCREEN

Just the photo

One key strips everything away but the image and its keeper star, with your place in the folder in the corner. Every shortcut still works.

TRASH

Nothing gets deleted

Non-keepers are moved, never removed, into one folder on your Desktop by default. Same-named photos from different cameras never overwrite each other.

OPEN WITH

Straight from Explorer

Right-click any photo and open it with Rack Viewer. It loads that photo's whole folder, ready to review from where you clicked.

OFFLINE

Your photos stay yours

No account, no upload, no telemetry — not one photo or video ever leaves your computer. The only thing that ever goes online is a one-time license activation, so it works exactly the same at camp with no signal.
