How it works
Open a folder. Keep the good ones. Bin the rest.

Sorting your photos by species is one job. Deciding which ones are actually worth keeping is another — and that one is still yours. Rack Viewer just makes it fast.

01 // BROWSE

Never wait on a photo

Photos are decoded ahead of where you are, in the direction you're going, so holding the arrow key feels instant even on full-size camera JPEGs.

02 // KEEP

One key per photo

Mark anything worth keeping. A gold star lands on the image and the file drops into the keeper list, so you always know where you stand. If a folder is mostly worth keeping, switch to discard mode and mark only the rejects instead — everything you leave unmarked stays exactly where it is.

03 // CLEAR OUT

Move the rest in one go

One Keeper Cleanup moves everything that isn't a keeper to one trash folder, grouped by where it came from — or in discard mode, just the ones you marked. Nothing is deleted; you empty that folder yourself when you're happy, and it goes to the Recycle Bin when you do.

A look at it
Everything on one screen

The photo as big as your monitor allows, your keepers listed beside it, and every count and shortcut along the bottom.

What's in it
Built for one job, done properly

Everything below works with no internet connection and no sign-in. The one exception is activating a license, which happens once.

VIDEO

Clips play with sound

Land on a clip and it starts playing on its own, so a card full of video reviews as fast as one full of photos. Play, pause, scrub, step a frame at a time and set the volume without leaving the window. Audio and picture stay locked together.

PICTURE

Zoom in, always the right way up

Ctrl and the scroll wheel zooms straight in on the pointer, and you can drag to pan — on a paused video frame too. Photos and clips arrive upright on their own, portrait phone video included. Anything that doesn't, one key turns — and on a photo that turn is written into the file itself, so it stays fixed in Explorer and anywhere else you send it.

FULL SCREEN

Just the photo

One key strips everything away but the image and its mark, with your place in the folder and which way round you're working shown in the corner. Every shortcut still works.

TRASH

Nothing goes without asking

Cleared-out files are moved, never removed, into one folder on your Desktop by default. Same-named photos from different cameras never overwrite each other. Emptying that folder is a separate, deliberate step, and it goes to the Recycle Bin — so even then it is recoverable.

OPEN WITH

Straight from Explorer

Right-click any photo and open it with Rack Viewer. It loads that photo's whole folder, ready to review from where you clicked.

OFFLINE

Your photos stay yours

No account, no upload, no telemetry — not one photo or video ever leaves your computer. The only thing that ever goes online is a one-time license activation, so it works exactly the same at camp with no signal.
