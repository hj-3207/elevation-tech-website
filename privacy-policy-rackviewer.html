Rack Viewer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This policy explains what information the Rack Viewer desktop application collects. Rack Viewer is developed by Elevation Technology Solutions ("we," "us"). If you have any questions, email elevationtechnology@outlook.com.

The short version: Rack Viewer collects nothing. It has no account, no licence key and no internet features at all. Your photos and video never leave your computer, because the app never sends anything anywhere.

Your photos and video stay on your computer

Rack Viewer opens the folder you point it at and displays what is in it. Everything happens entirely on your own computer. Your files are never uploaded to us or to any third party, and we never see them.

Information we collect

None. Rack Viewer has no sign-in, no licence activation, no analytics and no telemetry. It makes no network connections, so it works exactly the same with your internet disconnected.

What the app stores on your computer

Two things are written to your own machine, and neither is ever sent to us:

  • Your settings. Your preferences — the trash folder location, preview quality and playback volume — are saved to a settings file in your Windows user profile (%APPDATA%\RackViewer). Deleting that file resets the app to its defaults.
  • Files you move. When you move non-keepers to the trash, Rack Viewer moves those files into a folder you choose — it does not delete them, copy them elsewhere, or send them anywhere. Emptying that folder is up to you.

Who we share it with

Nobody. There is nothing to share, because the app collects nothing.

This website

Our website is a static site and does not collect personal information beyond the standard, anonymized server logs kept by our hosting provider.

One exception, and it is not about the app: when you click the download button on the Rack Viewer page, we record an anonymous, timestamped count (via Supabase) so we can gauge interest. That record contains no personal information — just the date and time of the click. It is kept separately from the equivalent count for our other apps. Supabase's privacy policy: supabase.com/privacy.

Children

Rack Viewer is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.
