How it works
Point it at your photos. Get sorted folders.

Drop in a season's worth of camera cards and let Rack Detector do the sorting — no manual scrubbing, no uploading your photos to the cloud.

01 // DETECT

Finds the animals

A detector locates every animal, person, and vehicle in each frame — and flags the empty ones as blanks so you can skip them.

02 // IDENTIFY

Identifies the species

On-device AI names the species and filters out anything that doesn't belong in North America, so your elk and deer land where they should.

03 // SORT

Sorts into folders

Every photo is copied into a per-species folder — elk, mule deer, whitetail, bird, bear, human, and more — ready to review in seconds.
